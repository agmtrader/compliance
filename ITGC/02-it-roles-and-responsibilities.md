# IT Roles, Responsibilities, and Reporting Procedure

## Purpose
Define IT roles, responsibilities, reporting lines, and required operating documents so IT activities are governed and auditable.

## System of Record
GitHub repository document control under `Compliance/ITGC/` in this repository.

## Scope
Applies to all personnel performing IT-related work, including employees and contractors.

## Core Roles
- Executive Sponsor: Hernan Castro (CEO)
- IT Owner: Andres Aguilar

## Responsibility Model
1. Executive Sponsor
- Approves IT governance documents and major changes.
- Reviews quarterly IT status summary.

2. IT Owner
- Maintains IT governance documentation.
- Assigns operational ownership for systems/processes.
- Reports quarterly status, risks, and overdue actions.

3. Process/System Owners
- Execute controls assigned to their systems/processes.
- Maintain required evidence for control execution.

## Required Governance Documents
The following must exist and be current in `Compliance/ITGC/`:
- IT strategy/governance procedure
- Information security policy
- Documentation standard/procedure
- Training procedure
- Change management procedure
- Logging/monitoring procedure
- Access control and SoD procedure
- Business continuity/disaster recovery procedure
- Data retention procedure

## Procedure
1. Role assignment
- IT Owner maintains a simple role register mapping each control/process to a named owner.

2. Quarterly governance review (calendar quarters)
- IT Owner checks whether each required governance document is:
  - present
  - assigned to an owner
  - reviewed within the last 12 months
- Gaps are logged with target dates.

3. Approval workflow
- New or updated governance documents are merged only via Pull Request.
- Hernan Castro approval is required for major governance documents and major changes.

4. Reporting
- IT Owner publishes a quarterly IT governance summary including:
  - document status
  - overdue reviews
  - open remediation items

## Minimum Evidence to Retain
- Role register with named owners.
- Quarterly governance review record.
- Pull Request approvals and merge history for governance document changes.
- Quarterly governance summary.

## Review Frequency
- Quarterly governance review (calendar quarters).
- Full role and document ownership review at least annually.
